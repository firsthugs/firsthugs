Returns & Refunds

  • Returns must be requested within 7 days of receiving your order.
  • For defective or incorrect items, please contact us with your order details and photos of the item.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, unworn, and with original tags attached.
  • Return shipping costs are covered by FirstHugs for defective or incorrect items. For other approved returns, return shipping is borne by the customer.
  • Refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item has been received and inspected.
